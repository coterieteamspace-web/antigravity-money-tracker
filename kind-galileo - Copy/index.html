Where is your data stored?

100% Client-Side Local Storage: All your transactions, settings, and monthly records are stored directly inside your computer browser's private sandbox storage (window.localStorage under the key aura_finance_data_v2).

🔒
Complete Privacy: Zero cloud servers, zero analytics, zero external network requests. Your financial figures never leave this computer.
💾
Browser Profile Location: Stored on your Windows disk inside your browser user directory (e.g. AppData\Local\Microsoft\Edge\User Data or Google\Chrome\User Data).
📤
Full Data Portability: Use the top right menu (⋮) anytime to export a spreadsheet-ready CSV or download a complete JSON backup to your desktop.
